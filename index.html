Frequently asked questions

A couple of quick answers. You can always reach us if you need more details.

Do guests need to download an app?
No. Guests only scan a QR code on the table. Cheflink opens in their browser and ties their orders to that table’s tab.
Can I run Cheflink in multiple locations?
Yes. Cheflink is multi-tenant by design, so you can manage multiple venues under one account with separate menus and staff.
Does Cheflink replace my POS?
Today Cheflink sits alongside your existing POS as a super-charged ordering and tab layer. Native POS integrations are on our roadmap.
